ChatDock Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 11, 2026

ChatDock ("the extension", "we") is a browser extension that adds folders, favorites, search, bookmarks, notes and bulk actions to chatgpt.com. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

1. Data stored on your device
ChatDock stores the following only on your own device, in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local):
- Folder names, colors, images and the chats you assign to each folder
- Favorites and interface settings
- Bookmarks, highlights, notes and saved prompts (these can include text you select from your chats)
- A cached copy of your chat list (chat IDs, titles and dates) so the sidebar opens quickly

This data is not sent to us or to any third party. We cannot see it.

2. Data accessed on chatgpt.com
To show your chat list, ChatDock reads your conversation list from chatgpt.com using your existing, logged-in session. When you archive, rename, move or delete a chat through ChatDock, that action is sent to chatgpt.com, the same as if you did it in ChatGPT itself.
To make these requests, the extension reads your ChatGPT session token from the page. The token is kept in memory for a few minutes only. It is never saved, logged or sent anywhere other than chatgpt.com.

3. What we do not do
- We do not collect, store or receive your data on any server
- We do not sell or share your data
- We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools
- We do not load or run remote code
- We do not use your data for anything other than the features described above

4. Exports
If you export your data or a chat (for example as JSON, TXT or PDF), the file is created on your device and saved where you choose. It is not uploaded anywhere.

5. Deleting your data
You can delete all stored data at any time using Settings > Clear data inside ChatDock, or by removing the extension from your browser.

6. Permissions
- storage / unlimitedStorage: save your folders, notes, settings and chat-list cache on your device
- Access to chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com: show the sidebar inside ChatGPT and read your own chat list

7. Children
ChatDock is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, for example if paid plans or license checks are added, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.

9. Not affiliated with OpenAI
ChatDock is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by OpenAI.
